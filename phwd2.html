The Empty Seat: A Haunting College Mystery

Maayli - A B.Tech Student ~~

In a well-known engineering college, there is a classroom where one seat has remained empty for over two years. It is located in the third row, last bench. Students avoid sitting there, not because it is uncomfortable, but because it is believed to belong to a former student named Maayli. Maayli was a B.Tech student known for her discipline and silence. She attended every lecture and always occupied the same seat. One semester, however, she suddenly stopped coming to college. There was no official announcement of her withdrawal, and no record of transfer. Her name simply disappeared from the attendance list, yet her presence seemed to remain within the classroom. Teachers began noticing unusual incidents. During lectures, Maayli’s name occasionally appeared on the digital attendance screen without being entered. In the computer lab, one system would turn on by itself, displaying incomplete lines of code that no student remembered writing. These events were dismissed as technical errors, but students felt uneasy. The mystery deepened when a new student once sat on the empty seat. She reported feeling an unnatural coldness and found a sentence written on the first page of her notebook: *“I tried. No one saw.”* Shocked and frightened, she left the classroom immediately. Later, a security guard revealed that Maayli had stayed late in the computer lab one night before her exams and was never seen again. Her academic records were cleared, and the laboratory was renovated, but her seat in the classroom remained untouched. Today, students claim that at exactly 9:00 a.m., the chair moves slightly backward, as if someone has just stood up. In the silent lab, keyboards are heard typing when no one is present. A message sometimes appears on screens: **“Don’t disappear like I did.”** The empty seat still remains in the classroom—a reminder that not all stories end with graduation. Some remain, quietly haunting the halls of learning.
