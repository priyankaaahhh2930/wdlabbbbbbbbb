Natasha Romanoff

The Black Widow • Avenger • Hero

About

Natasha Romanoff, also known as Black Widow, is one of the most skilled spies and assassins in the Marvel Universe. Trained in the Red Room, she overcame her past to become a founding member of the Avengers and a true hero.

Legacy

Known for her intelligence, combat skills, and unwavering courage, Natasha played a crucial role in saving the world multiple times. Her ultimate sacrifice proved that heroes are defined by their choices, not their past.

"I've got red in my ledger. I'd like to wipe it out."

Why We Remember Her

Natasha Romanoff showed us that redemption is possible, strength comes from within, and true bravery often goes unseen. She remains an inspiration to millions.
